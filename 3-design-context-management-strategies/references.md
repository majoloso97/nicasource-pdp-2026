# Main References
- [Prompt Engineering](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2023-03-15-prompt-engineering/): Lilian Weng’s overview of prompt engineering techniques including few-shot prompting, chain-of-thought, self-consistency and reasoning strategies
- [Large Language Models Are Human-Level Prompt Engineers](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.01910): Research paper introducing Automatic Prompt Engineer (APE), where LLMs generate and optimize prompts automatically through evaluation and search
- [Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172): Study on long-context LLM behavior showing that models struggle to retrieve information placed in the middle of large contexts
- [ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.03629): Foundational paper introducing the reasoning + acting loop used in modern AI agents and tool-using workflows
